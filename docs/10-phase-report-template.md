## Part D — Recommended team recording workflow

1. Before the interview, assign a participant ID and explain the research purpose.
2. Obtain voluntary participation consent. Ask separately before audio recording or photography.
3. Conduct the interview in English using the appropriate questionnaire.
4. One team member asks questions while recording written notes. A second member may assist if the participant is comfortable.
5. Immediately after the interview, transfer the responses into Google Forms.
6. Compare the digital entry with the written notes to avoid missing or changing details.
7. Store written notes and digital records securely, using participant IDs.
8. Review responses together to identify recurring problems, differences, and useful quotes.

## Part E — Quality check before accepting an interview record

- [ ] The interview was conducted with a real participant.
- [ ] The participant belongs to the selected target group.
- [ ] Consent was obtained.
- [ ] The actual answers are recorded, not assumed answers.
- [ ] Written notes are complete enough to understand the conversation.
- [ ] The Google Forms entry matches the written notes.
- [ ] At least one useful direct quote has been recorded, if available.
- [ ] Personal identifying information has not been unnecessarily collected.
- [ ] Interviewer interpretations are kept separate from participant responses.

**Research integrity:** Never fill missing answers with guesses. If a participant does not know, does not remember, or declines to answer, record that accurately.
